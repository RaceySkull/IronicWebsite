How it works

Three steps, start to finish. Your hosting and your files stay yours the whole time.

  1. 01

    Open your site

    Upload your site’s folder or a ZIP. Orbyt reads your pages, styles, fonts and photos, and checks for anything missing.

  2. 02

    Change anything

    Click to select, drag to move, double-click to type. Right-click any shape to turn it into a button, a countdown or a pop-up.

  3. 03

    Download and go live

    See every change in plain words, then download a ZIP of just what changed. Upload it to your host and you’re live.

Questions

Does it cost anything?

It’s free while Orbyt Designs is in early access.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. If you can use a design app or make a slideshow, you can use Orbyt. There’s a Code view for people who want it, but you never have to open it.

What kind of sites does it work with?

Sites made of plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript files, like most sites hosted on GitHub Pages.

Where does my site live?

Wherever it lives now. Orbyt edits your files and gives them back; you upload them to your own host.

Who can see my team’s work?

Only people you invite. Each team’s site, files and settings are kept separate and private. See the privacy page for exactly what we store.

Ready to open your site?

Bring the site you already have. Your first change takes about a minute.
