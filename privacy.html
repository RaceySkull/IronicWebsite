Privacy

Orbyt Designs is a tool for editing your team’s website. We keep as little about you as we can.

What we store

  • Your email address, so we can send you sign-in links.
  • The display name you choose, so teammates know who changed what.
  • Which teams you’re on and your role on each one.
  • Your team’s website files (pages, styles, photos and documents) and a plain-words list of changes, like “Changed the text size of the headline.”

What we don’t do

  • We don’t sell or share your information, show ads, or track you around the web.
  • We don’t ask for your age, address, phone number or anything else.

Who can see your team’s work

Only people on your team. Each team’s files and settings are kept separate by rules in the database itself, so other teams can’t see them.

Where it’s kept

Accounts and files are stored with Supabase, a hosting provider. Sign-in emails are sent through it too.

Removing your information

Leave a team from its Settings tab at any time, and the team’s owner can remove members. To delete your account completely, contact the person who runs your Orbyt Designs team.

Age

You must be 13 or older to make an account.
